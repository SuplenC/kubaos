## `Sheet`

Rust `sheet::sheet`; QML `Sheet`. Its mockup page is the source of truth, with `PanelPage`, the template inside it: Templates, Sheet and PanelPage, in `design/mockups/`.

A card floating on the desktop: the shell's control center, clock, system monitor, notifications, the power menu and the OSD. The screen has no frame round it ([Bar](bar.md), "Layouts"), and every card is the shell's glass, a surface of its own over the desktop (`design/mockups/src/lib/glass.ts`): `surface` at the glass setting over the compositor's blur, its highlight, its ring and its shadow (`docs/DESIGN.md`, "Opacities" and "Elevation"). The desktop never dims under a card. A card is as large as its content, which keeps its own padding, its corners `radius.surface`, the same as a window's, and the boxes inside it `radius.content` with `space.s16` round them, so they run concentric with it. It opens `size.gap` under the bar, centred on whole device pixels under the bar item that opened it and stopped `size.gap` from the screen's sides; one with no item stands at the screen's right, or in its middle for the clock's. The item carries the bar's edge light, and the card echoes it along its top edge between its corners; the clock's light lies under the half whose view is open. Tall content reaches `size.gap` above the screen's bottom edge, and then its body scrolls. A card may instead stand `size.gap` above the middle of the screen's bottom edge (the power menu, the share picker's bar, the OSD), its corner concentric with what it holds. Several cards share one screen, each where its owner puts it. Open and at rest, a card cuts nothing its content draws past its edge: a lit control's glow, a hint or a tooltip over a control in its top row shows whole, cut only by the screen; while it opens or closes, and while twins swap their content, it cuts at its edge, so nothing sliding shows past it.

Opening, a card fades in as it settles `space.s8` down into place over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, its content riding with it; it leaves over `medium` on `emphasized_accel`. Cards under the bar's end that open from each other are twins (the system monitor, the notifications panel and the control center, their buttons in that order along the bar): one asked for while another is open takes the card over, which moves and resizes to its place under the new item over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, while the edge light travels with it, its leading edge over `medium` and its trailing one over `long`. A third asked for mid-swap takes over from the one arriving, and the one still leaving goes at once. The old content slides `space.s24` away from the new one's button and fades over `micro`; the new one slides in from its button's side over `short` on `emphasized_decel`, `duration.swap_lag` later, and fades in over `duration.swap_fade` once the old is gone, so the two are never drawn over each other, about 250 ms in all. Asked for again before the new content has begun to show, the old content turns back from where it is over `short`. With reduce motion nothing moves: a card stands open where it goes and fades in, surface and content together, over `short` on `emphasized_decel`, and fades out over `short` on `emphasized_accel` before it closes; asked for again while fading out, it fades back in from where it is, and between twins the content swaps at once. Every card in the shell does this, since the library's sheet does it.

*Not built yet: the shell's `Sheet` grows out of an inner edge of a screen frame and is one surface with it, `surface` at the frame's glass (`opacity.frame`, 0.94): where it meets the edge it curves into it through concave fillets of the surface radius, and its far corners take the surface radius. A corner sheet hangs from the frame's top right corner (the `PopupStack` and the bell's peek), and a right-edge sheet may start below the bar, as the critical popups under an open panel do. It slides out from under the frame as it opens, over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, and twins keep the one surface where it is while only the content swaps. An open panel dims the desktop at `opacity.panel_scrim`. QML's `Sheet` cuts its content at its edge open too, 4 short of a lit card's glow at the container's padding. The owner draws its surface from its `path` at its `fade`, in one layer with the frame's, so the two never double up; `kubaos-ui` draws one open, hanging from the edge above it. The mockups draw the card.*
