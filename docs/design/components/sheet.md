## `Sheet`

Rust `sheet::sheet`; QML `Sheet`.

A panel growing out of an inner edge of the screen frame: the shell's control center, clock, system monitor, power menu, wallpaper picker, OSD and notification stacks. The sheet and the frame are one surface, `surface` at the frame's glass (0.94): where the sheet meets the edge it curves into it through concave fillets of the surface radius, the frame's own inner corner, and its far corners take the surface radius. It is as large as its content, which keeps its own padding, and sits along the edge where its owner puts it, centred on whole device pixels by default. In the shell it slides out from under the frame as it opens, over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, its content riding on its far edge and fading in a beat after the surface moves; it leaves over `medium` on `emphasized_accel`. Sheets in the same place are twins (the system monitor, the notifications panel and the control center, their buttons in that order along the bar): one asked for while another is open takes the surface over where it is, and only the content changes. A third asked for mid-swap takes over from the one arriving, and the one still leaving goes at once. The old content slides `space.s24` away from the new one's button and fades over `micro`; the new one slides in from its button's side over `short` on `emphasized_decel`, starting half a `micro` later and fading in once the old is gone, so the two are never drawn over each other, about 250 ms in all. Asked for again before the new content has begun to show, the old content turns back from where it is over `short`. With reduce motion the content swaps at once. The owner draws its surface from its `path`, in one layer with the frame's, so the two never double up; `kubaos-ui` draws one open, hanging from the edge above it.
