## `NotificationGroup`

QML `NotificationGroup`. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, NotificationGroup, in `design/mockups/`. *Not built yet: the shell draws it as `CardGroup` in `config/quickshell/modules/notifications`, not a library part, and casts `shadow.tucked` and `shadow.lifted` rather than the ladder's steps; those two tokens go once it moves. Its edges are flat `surface_container` with the card above's cast over each, and the newest card's `FocusRing` glows over them, so a lit stack at night barely reads as one. QML needs: each edge a raised step-1 part (`surface_container_high` at night, the rim), only the lowest casting its step's shadow, the card above's contact layer cast onto each edge by day alone, cut to the edge's rounded shape rather than a rectangle, the stack's one shadow under every card, and the edges drawn over the newest card's glow at rest, each cut to what the card above leaves showing.*

One app's notifications as one thing, in the `PopupStack`, the bell's peek and the notifications panel, each a `NotificationCard`. A single notification is the card alone.

- **At rest, a stack:** the newest card, standing for the whole group (its count chip, and its dismiss and actions take them all), the others tucked under it. One narrower edge peeks out below it for a pair, two for more, each `space.s8` lower and `space.s8` narrower each side than the one above, each a raised part on the ladder's step 1, so it reads against the panel lit or not: at night one container role above the card (`surface_container_high`) with the rim, by day the card's fill with the rim and the card above's contact layer cast onto it, cut to its rounded shape. The stack casts one step-1 shadow from its lowest edge, under every card, so no peek sinks under another's. At rest the edges draw over the newest card, each over the one under it, showing only what the card above leaves (the band under its straight foot and the slivers under its rounded corners): a lit card's glow runs round the stack and never washes over the cards behind it. The edges' room is part of the group's height, so nothing overlaps.
- **A preview, under a resting pointer:** after the pointer has rested `medium` on it, up to three cards spread down into whole cards `space.s8` apart over what is behind, each on the ladder's step 2 whole as it spreads: its fill one container role up at night (`surface_container_high`), and every part filled from a container role inside it (its count chip, its icon tile) one role up with it, so each stays a step above the card; its rim, and its own shadow straight under it, rising from step 1 to step 2; "+N more · click to open" in `small` and `primary` sits `space.s8` under them when there are more. What holds the group fades every other row toward its surface by `opacity.recede`, and those the preview covers go entirely; nothing moves. The preview's whole area, the gaps between its cards and the line under them included, is the group's for the pointer, growing and shrinking with the cards, so moving from one spread card to another never leaves it. Leaving tucks the cards back, once the hover has settled (`duration.instant`, a turn of the event loop), so a card passing under the pointer as it moves doesn't let go. The keyboard never previews.
- **Opened:** every card takes its place, `space.s8` apart, under a heading a small control tall: the app's name in `label`, a `Chip` with the count, and Show less, a Text sm button, at its end. The group grows, so only what is below it moves; each card is then on its own.

**Motion.** The preview spreads over `medium` on `standard_decel` and tucks in over `short` on `standard_accel`; opening takes `medium` on `standard_decel`, closing `short` on `standard_accel`. Each card's face fades in once its edge has spread; the heading fades in over the second half of the opening. With reduce motion each state comes at once and the cards fade in over `short`.

**Keys**, from what holds it: → or l opens it, the keyboard on its first card; ← or h closes it; ↑ and ↓ move card to card inside it opened and leave it past its ends, ↑ into an opened group landing on its last card; Enter or Space opens a stack and unfolds an opened card; Del dismisses the card, or a stack's whole group; Esc folds an unfolded card, then closes the group. A click on the newest card or anywhere on the preview opens it; Show less, or a click on it again, closes it, and a closed group under the pointer doesn't preview again until the pointer has left.
