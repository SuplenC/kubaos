# SidebarPage and SidebarRow

A template: a screen's layout, empty of any app's content ([DESIGN.md, "Levels and where they live"](../../DESIGN.md#levels-and-where-they-live)). [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every part keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to. The specimen's Templates page shows it in its states and, in the window, live: resize the window and drive it with the keys (Ctrl+Tab leaves the page).

## `SidebarPage`

Rust `template::sidebar_page`; *iced only: it is an app's window, and apps are `kubaos-ui` windows.*

An app's window as its sections in a sidebar beside a page that scrolls, with Home, a page that is only search, at the top. Settings is built on it.

**What the template owns, and what the app does.** The template owns where the person is and how they move: which section shows and which of its sub-sections the page is scrolled to, where the keyboard is, the keys, the find box and its matches, and the motion between pages. The app describes its pages (`Outline`: Home's words, then each section's symbol, title, accent, lede and sub-sections, and how many rows each well of a sub-section holds), draws each row when asked, answers whether a row matches what is typed in the find box (`Content::matches`), and runs Home's search: the template tells it what is typed (`Event::Query`), it gives back how many results there are (`set_results`) and draws them under the field, and on Enter or a press on a result (`Event::Open`; a result sends `Message::Open`) it names the row to go to (`land`). Enter and Space on a row of the page are the app's (`Event::Press`, `Event::Toggle`). A press on a row sends `Message::Point`, so the keyboard picks up where the pointer was. The app runs the template's `subscription`, and calls `measure` as the window opens and after anything on a page changes its height (a row that opens, a note that appears); the template measures again itself as a section opens and as the window resizes.

**The sidebar.** A band 264 wide (`sidebar`) in `surface_container_low`, full height, its rows 12 in and 4 apart. Home is its first row, then a hairline 8 clear of the rows on either side, then the sections. The current section's or Home's row is filled (`surface_container_high`) and its symbol is in `primary`. Only the current section opens its sub-sections, as smaller rows under it. Beside the current sub-section a short `primary` bar with the neon glow says where the page is: it glides from row to row (`medium`, `standard`) as the page scrolls and on a press, and a section changing puts it in place at once. When the sidebar runs past the window it scrolls on its own, its rows staying 44, and keeps the keyboard's row in view with its 12 around it.

**The page.** The section's header (accent, title and its lede, `Header`), then each sub-section's heading (`Section`) over its wells of rows (`List`, 16 apart), the parts 40 apart, in a `Page` that scrolls. Pressing a sub-section in the sidebar scrolls its heading to the page's lead, 32 below the top, and the sidebar follows the page as it scrolls (the scroll-spy): the current sub-section is the last whose heading has reached that line, or the last of all once the page can scroll no further. A sub-section pressed stays current while the page stays where it was scrolled to for it, though it may be too short to reach the line.

**Home.** A centred column, 560 at most (`column`): Home's header, the search field across every setting, and the app's results under it, 24 apart. An empty field shows the app's sections.

**The find box.** `/` or Ctrl+F opens it on a section's page (on Home they go to the search field). It floats in the page's top-right corner, 16 below the top and the container padding in from the right, 320 wide (`find`), on a `Popover` with the panel's shadow, and takes no layout. It holds a field and, once something is typed, "n of m" in `mono`. It searches that page only: the current match's row is where the keyboard is, scrolled into view below the box, and the sidebar's bar follows into its sub-section. Enter or ↓ goes to the next match, Shift+Enter or ↑ to the previous, wrapping round. Esc closes it, scrolls the page back to where it was, with the sub-section that was current, and puts the keyboard back where it was. With no match, `KeyHints` under the field offer Enter to search every setting from Home: Enter goes Home with the query in its field.

**How rows show the find box.** Each row is told how to show (`RowState`): `selected` (the keyboard is on it), `found` (`Off`, `Match` with its letters lit, `Miss` dimmed, `Current` with the landed highlight), the `query` to light, and `landed`, the landed highlight's strength as a row is reached from Home's search, fading over `slow`. The row draws them as its own look says. *Not drawn yet: `ListRow` has no lit letters, no dimmed state and no landed highlight (#304), so the specimen's stand-in rows show only `selected`.*

**Motion.** Changing section fades the page in and rises it 8 over `short` (`emphasized_decel`); reduce motion swaps it. The find box arrives and goes with a fade and an 8 drop over `short`; reduce motion keeps the fade. Nothing redraws at rest: the template asks for frames only while something moves, and the bar redraws itself while it glides, without the view being built again.

**The keyboard.** The window opens on Home, the keyboard in its field. Tab and Shift+Tab go round search → sidebar on Home, and find box (while open) → sidebar → page on a section. Off a field, the arrows and the Neovim keys move: in the sidebar ↑ ↓, j k, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+J and Ctrl+K go through Home, the sections and the open section's sub-sections, wrapping round, and showing what they land on; Home and End go to the first and last row, Page Up and Page Down by as many rows as show whole; → , l or Enter go into the page, at the first row of the current sub-section (into the field, on Home). In the page the same keys move through the rows, wrapping round, each brought into view with 24 around it; Home, End, Page Up and Page Down stop at the ends; ←, h or Esc go back to the sidebar, onto the row's sub-section. Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down go to the section before or after from anywhere, Home included, wrapping round. In Home's field ↑ ↓ and the Ctrl keys move through the results, Enter goes to the one the keyboard is on, Esc clears the field and a second Esc goes to the sidebar. Ctrl+Tab is left to the window. A press selects quietly: the focus tube shows only when the keyboard moved there.

**Size.** From the 720 × 480 minimum (`app_min_width`, `app_min_height`) to a full screen, the sidebar keeps its 264 and the page takes the rest, its padding the container's for its own width: 16 on the 456 a minimal window leaves it, 24 from 640, 32 from 1024.

## `SidebarRow`

Rust `template::sidebar_page` draws it; it is part of `SidebarPage`, not a component of its own.

A row of the sidebar, the control radius, its words one line that ends in an ellipsis. `section` (Home's too): 44 (`row`), a 20 symbol, `body` 12 after it. `sub`: 32 (`control_sm`), `small`, its words starting where a section's do. Its states: resting in `on_surface_variant`; current, in `on_surface` (and filled, its symbol in `primary`, on a section); hovered and pressed with the state layer; focused from the keyboard with the focus tube.
