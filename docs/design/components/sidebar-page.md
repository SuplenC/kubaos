# SidebarPage and SidebarRow

A template: a screen's layout, empty of any app's content ([DESIGN.md, "Levels and where they live"](../../DESIGN.md#levels-and-where-they-live)). [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every part keeps.

## `SidebarPage`

Rust `template::sidebar_page`; iced only: it is an app's window, and apps are `kubaos-ui` windows. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Templates, SidebarPage, in `design/mockups/`, live in a window that resizes from the app's minimum to a 1080p screen.

An app's window as its sections in a sidebar beside a page that scrolls, with Home, a page that is only search, at the top. Settings is built on it.

**What the template owns, and what the app does.** The template owns where the person is and how they move: which section shows and which of its sub-sections the page is scrolled to, where the keyboard is, the keys, the find box and its matches, and the motion between pages. The app gives it its outline (Home's words, then each section's symbol, title, accent, lede and sub-sections, each a heading, a line and its wells of rows), and runs Home's search through Kotodama's engine. A section may draw a region of its own in place of its sub-sections' wells, holding one `Section` per sub-section in order: CanvasPage is one.

**The sidebar.** A band `size.sidebar` (264) wide in `surface_container_low`, full height, its rows `space.s12` in and `space.s4` apart. Home is its first row, then a hairline (`Divider`) `space.s8` clear of the rows on either side, then the sections. The current section's row, or Home's, is filled in `surface_container_high`, its symbol `primary`: the fill is one highlight laid under the rows, which carries the row's tube while the row is lit (hovered, or the keyboard on it). Only the current section opens its sub-sections, as `SidebarRow`s `sub` under it; every section's list is there, folded to nothing but the current one's, and the rows are made once, so a change of section moves them rather than drawing them again, and hover and focus stay where they are. When the sidebar runs past the window it scrolls on its own (`Scroll`), its rows staying `size.row`, the band's padding its thumb's room, and keeps the keyboard's row in view with `space.s12` round it.

**The bar.** Beside the current sub-section a short bar of neon says where the page is: `type.small`'s line (16) tall and the tabs' rail thick (`tabs.rail`, `tabs.rail_day` by day, whole device pixels), centred in the symbols' column. At night it is the core (`primary_core`) with the content glow round it (`focus.content_glow` over `focus.content_near`); by day the tone's glass, `primary_glass`, with none. It glides from row to row over `medium` on `standard` as the page scrolls and on a press. While a page of a section's own is open (a row's sub-page), the bar stays beside the sub-section it was opened from. Each section's list has its own, on its first sub-section, which goes and comes with its list as it folds and unfolds, never flying across the rows.

**The page.** The section's `Header` (accent, title and its pun lede, and the small button the app may put at the end of the title's line), then each sub-section, a `Section` with its heading over its wells of rows (`List`, `space.s16` apart), the parts `space.s32` apart, in a `Page` that scrolls (`Scroll`), its content `size.canvas_wide` (1000) wide at most from its start, so on a wide screen a name and its control stay a glance apart, its thumb in the page's own padding at its end with no gutter. Pressing a sub-section in the sidebar glides the page's scroll over `medium` on `standard` until its heading is at the lead line, `space.s32` below the page's top. As the page scrolls the sidebar follows it: the current sub-section is the last whose heading has reached the lead line, or the last of all once a page that scrolls can scroll no further. A sub-section pressed stays current while the page stays where it went for it, though the page may stop short of the line.

**Home.** A centred column, `size.column` (560) at most: Home's header, the search field across every setting, and under it, `space.s24` apart, the sections while the field is empty, or the settings that match, each a two-line `ListRow`, those whose name matches first, then those whose line or tip holds what was typed as a whole run, a tip's keys read by their names ("super tab"), and a sub-section whose own tip holds it, its result the sub-section's name over its section's, landing on its first row: its name with the typed letters lit, its path under it ("Appearance › Theme") and its value at its end. The window opens on Home, the keyboard in the field. ↑ ↓ and Ctrl+J, Ctrl+K, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+P move through the results; Enter, or a press on a result, goes to the setting: its section, scrolled to its row, the keyboard on it and the row lit by the landed highlight (the accent at `opacity.tint` laid over the row), which fades over `slow` on `standard`. With nothing found a line under the field says so. Esc clears the field; a second Esc goes to the sidebar.

**The find box.** `/` or Ctrl+F on a section's page (on Home they go to the field) opens it in the page's top-right corner, `space.s16` below the top and the container padding in from the end: a `Popover` `size.find` (320) wide, raised to step 2, over the content and taking no layout. It holds a field and its count, "n of m" in `mono`, whose room is kept while nothing is typed. It searches that page only, as Home does (a row's name, then its line and its tip): matching letters lit, rows that don't match at `opacity.miss`, the current match the keyboard's row with the landed highlight held, scrolled into view below the box, the sidebar's bar following into its sub-section. Enter or ↓ goes to the next match, Shift+Enter or ↑ to the previous, round from either end. Esc closes it, puts the page back where it was, with the sub-section and the row that were current, and the keyboard where it was. With no match, `KeyHints` under the field offer Enter to search every setting from Home, the query carried over into Home's field.

**Motion.** A change of section glides in the sidebar: the highlight slides from the old row to the new over `medium` on `standard` (never a curve that overshoots), its tube with it, lit as it was, so nothing goes out on one row and strikes on the next; the old list folds and the new one unfolds over the same `medium` on `standard`, their height and their opacity, so the rows under them slide, and the highlight lands on the row as the row lands. The old and new rows' words and symbols cross-fade over the same time. A change asked for meanwhile carries on from where the highlight and the lists are. The rows move under a pointer that stays where it was, so the row it is over then doesn't light until the pointer moves. With reduce motion the lists change at once and the highlight fades in on its row over `short` on `standard`.

Every change of what the page shows is a page turn, which moves the page, its title with it, as a panel swapped for its twin moves. The page shown leaves `space.s24` toward one side and fades out over `micro` on `emphasized_accel`; the next is drawn as it has gone, at its top, and comes in `space.s24` from the other side, fading in over `short` on `emphasized_decel`, so the two never lie over each other and a change of section takes about 300 ms. The next page waits hidden where its arrival starts until it has been drawn once, so the costlier first frame of a page never eats its arrival. Along the sidebar a section lower in the list rises in from below while the page left goes up, and a higher one comes down; Home is the top of the list. A page of a section's own (a row with a chevron, which the app draws in its region through the template's `turn`) comes in sideways from the end while the section leaves toward the start, and Back turns the other way, the section scrolled where it was. A setting landed on from Home's search or the Command menu turns in already scrolled to its row, and its landed highlight plays once the page has arrived. A turn asked for while one plays retargets it: a page still leaving goes on leaving and the latest one asked for comes in; a page still coming in leaves from where it is. The sidebar's selection changes at once. While the page moves its room clips it, so the scroll's extent and thumb, the sidebar and the window hold still. Reduce motion makes a turn a fade: the page left goes at once and the next fades in over `short` on `standard`; the speed setting divides every duration. Picking a sub-section of the section shown is no turn: the page glides there. The find box arrives and goes with a fade and a `space.s8` drop over `short` on `standard`; reduce motion keeps the fade. The bar's glide and a press's scroll go over `medium` on `standard`, at once with reduce motion. Nothing redraws at rest.

**The keyboard.** Tab and Shift+Tab go round the window's stops: on Home its field and the sidebar; on a section the find box while it is open, the sidebar, then the page's stops (a well's rows are one stop, on the well holding the keyboard's row). In the sidebar ↑ ↓, j k, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+J and Ctrl+K go through Home, the sections and the current section's sub-sections, round from either end, showing what they land on; Home and End go to the first and last row, Page Up and Page Down by as many rows as show whole. →, l or Enter go into the page, at the first row of the current sub-section (into the field, on Home). In the page the same keys move through the rows, round from either end, each brought into view with `space.s24` round it; Home, End, Page Up and Page Down stop at the ends; Enter and Space do what the row does; ←, h or Esc go back to the sidebar, onto the row's sub-section. Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down go to the section before or after from anywhere, Home included, round from either end. A press selects quietly: the focus tube shows only when the keyboard moved there.

**Size.** From the 720 × 480 minimum (`app_min_width`, `app_min_height`) to a full screen, the sidebar keeps its `size.sidebar` and the page takes the rest, its padding the container's for its own width: 16 on the 456 a minimal window leaves it, 24 from 640, 32 from 1024. There is no icon rail at any width.

*Not built yet: `kubaos-ui` has no templates. A first build of `template::sidebar_page` waits for this mockup's approval; `ListRow` has no landed highlight and no miss state, `Field` no 48-tall size, and there is no home symbol.*

## `SidebarRow`

Part of `SidebarPage`, not a component of its own; it reports itself as `SidebarRow`, `section` or `sub`, its height fixed.

A row of the sidebar at the control radius (the window's `radius.surface` less the band's `space.s12`, so the two are concentric), its words one line that ends in an ellipsis. `section` (Home's too): `size.row` (44) tall, `space.s12` inside its sides, a `body` symbol `space.s12` before its words in `body`. `sub`: `size.control_sm` (32) tall in `small`, its words starting where a section's do.

Its states are a Text button's: at rest its words and symbol are `on_surface_variant`, with no fill; current, its words are `on_surface`, and a current section is filled in `surface_container_high`, its symbol `primary`. Lit, under the pointer or with the keyboard on it, the tube burns inside its edge in the accent with the glow outside at night (glass by day, `primary_glass`), and its words go to `on_surface`, over `short` on `standard`. Pressed, the tube runs white-hot to the core over `micro`.
