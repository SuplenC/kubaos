# StepRailPage

A template: [DESIGN.md, "Levels and where they live"](../../DESIGN.md#levels-and-where-they-live) says what a template is. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Templates, StepRailPage, in `design/mockups/`.

## `StepRailPage`

A step rail beside a page with a footer band, and how it moves between its pages. One template with two hosts: in an app's window (the updater) the rail and the page fill the window, on `surface`; in Kōbō they are its glass panel over the wallpaper and the scrim (`surface` at `opacity.glass` over the blurred wallpaper, the soft hairline, `radius.surface`, `shadow.panel`), inset 32 from a screen two columns wide (16 from a smaller one), centred, and no wider than the rail beside two columns with 40 either side.

**The rail** is `size.rail` (236) wide, 16 in above and below and 12 at its sides, on the well's fill (`surface_container_low` at `opacity.well`): the brand (Kōbō's lockup over its version line) or the window's title, 20, the steps back to back (`Step` in its rail box, each across the rail's whole inner width, a mark at its end), then room, 16 at least, and at its foot the keys nothing on the page shows, each a line of `KeyHints` (its words 8 after its keys), 4 apart: in Kōbō ↑ ↓ pick and Tab next field (Enter and Esc go, since Continue and Back show them), and on a running page with no band, Enter show the log; then, in Kōbō, 16 and the power button (its symbol on the steps' symbols' edge) and the battery. Where the steps don't fit they scroll between the brand and the hints, cut at their own box, and the current step is kept in view. A done step shows its check and takes a press to go back, as far as the first step after one that can't be undone, and none while a step runs; the current step is a quiet fill (`on_surface` at `opacity.hover`) with its symbol lit, so Continue stays the only loud thing; a step that failed shows `error`.

**The wire** runs down the steps' symbols, one tube `step_rail.wire` (3) thick centred on them on whole device pixels, from the first symbol's centre to the last's. Unlit it is `outline_variant`; lit, from the first symbol down to the current one's, it is `primary`, with a lit control's glow round it at night (`focus.near` and `focus.far`, while the signs are on) and none by day. Each symbol is a bead on it: the wire and its glow are cut away in a disc `step_rail.bead` (3) wider than the symbol on every side, so the wire passes behind the symbol and the fill under it (the rail's, or the current row's) shows round it. Done steps' symbols and the current one's are `primary`, the current one glowing at night; steps not reached keep their ink; while the current step runs, its symbol hums (`Step`).

**The page** is a clear `Page`: its body takes the height left, then 16 and the footer band, with **no hairline**: Back (`Text`, `arrow_back`) on the left, its symbol on the page's text edge (its own padding reaching past it), the one `Filled` button on the right (`arrow_forward` after its label), 16 apart, and a `small` note just before it; while something blocks Continue, its reason after the `error` symbol, Continue disabled, the reason flashing in `error` as Enter is refused and settling over `slow`. A page whose action is in its body, or that runs, has no band.

**Between pages** the new page slides 32 in from the direction of travel and fades in over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, on whole device pixels; the page left goes at once. The rail's rows change at once; the wire's lit length grows to the new current symbol (or retracts to it) over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, its end on whole device pixels, and each symbol it reaches lights over `short` as the light gets there (the new current one from `medium` in), one it leaves going out as the light leaves it. Reduce motion makes the page a fade and the wire and symbols change at once.

**Keys:** Enter is the page's `Filled` action; Esc goes back a page; ↑ ↓ (k j) move between the rail's pressable steps; Tab goes rail, page, Back, Continue. A page arriving puts the keyboard on its first thing to do.

*Not built yet: neither library has the template; Kōbō draws its rail, panel and footer by hand (`crates/kubaos-kobo/src/view.rs`), and the updater doesn't exist.*
