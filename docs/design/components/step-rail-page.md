# StepRailPage

A template: [DESIGN.md, "Levels and where they live"](../../DESIGN.md#levels-and-where-they-live) says what a template is. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Templates, StepRailPage, in `design/mockups/`.

## `StepRailPage`

A step rail beside a page with a footer band, and how it moves between its pages. One template with two hosts: in an app's window (the updater) the rail and the page fill the window, on `surface`; in Kōbō they are the whole screen, with no panel round them, on a [Backdrop](backdrop.md)'s glass (the wallpaper blurred under `surface` at `opacity.glass`), inset 32 from a screen two columns wide (16 from a smaller one) at its top and sides and 12 from its foot, so the band and the rail's foot sit low and the page takes the height, centred, and no wider than the rail beside two columns with 40 either side.

**The rail** is `size.rail` (236) wide, 16 in above and below and 12 at its sides (in Kōbō 24 above, and the page none above, so the page's title stands level with the rail's lockup and its kicker sits in the room over them), on the well's fill (`surface_container_low` at `opacity.well`) in a window, and in Kōbō on the glass alone, so nothing draws a box on the screen: the brand (Kōbō's lockup over its version line) or the window's title, 20, the steps back to back (`Step` in its rail box, each across the rail's whole inner width, a mark at its end), then room, 16 at least, and at its foot the keys nothing on the page shows, each a line of `KeyHints` (its words 8 after its keys), 4 apart: in Kōbō ↑ ↓ pick and Tab next field (Enter and Esc go, since Continue and Back show them), and on a running page with no band, Enter show the log; then, in Kōbō, 16 and the power button (its symbol on the steps' symbols' edge) and the battery. Where the steps don't fit they scroll between the brand and the hints, cut at their own box, and the current step is kept in view. A done step shows its check and takes a press to go back, as far as the first step after one that can't be undone, and none while a step runs; the current step is a quiet fill (`on_surface` at `opacity.hover`) with its symbol lit, so Continue stays the only loud thing; a step that failed shows `error`.

**The wire** runs down the steps' symbols as one neon sign: a length of tube `step_rail.wire` (3) thick between each symbol and the next, centred on them on whole device pixels, stopping `step_rail.bead` (3) short of both with round ends, so the tube runs into each symbol and never crosses a glyph. Unlit it is `outline_variant`; its light, `primary`, fills each length as far as it has come, from the first symbol down to the current one's, with a lit control's glow round it at night (`focus.near` and `focus.far`, while the signs are on) and none by day. The symbols are lit as the tube is: done steps' symbols and the current one's are `primary` and glow as the tube does at night; steps not reached are unlit, in the muted ink (`on_surface_variant`) beside the unlit tube; while the current step runs, its symbol hums (`Step`). Done, current and to do read apart by eye: a lit symbol with its check, a lit symbol on the quiet fill, an unlit symbol with nothing at its end.

**The page** is a clear `Page`: its body takes the height left, then 16 and the footer band (in Kōbō 24, which what scrolls reaches through under its foot fade, so nothing scrolling comes nearer the band's buttons than 24 and its end rests 24 above them; the band sits on the rail's foot line, its buttons centred on the rail's last line, the last key hint or the power button), with **no hairline**: Back (`Text`, `arrow_back`) on the left, its symbol on the page's text edge (its own padding hanging past it, the [button bar](button-bar.md)'s rule), the one `Filled` button on the right (`arrow_forward` after its label), 16 apart, and a `small` note just before it; while something blocks Continue, its reason after the `error` symbol, Continue disabled, the reason flashing in `error` as Enter is refused and settling over `slow`. A note or a reason longer than the room between the buttons breaks between words, the band growing with it, and pushes neither button. A page whose action is in its body, or that runs, has no band.

**Between pages** the new page slides 32 in from the direction of travel and fades in over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, on whole device pixels; in a window the page left goes at once. In Kōbō, where the page is the screen, the page left goes too: it slides 32 on, away from where the next comes in, and fades out over `short` on `emphasized_accel`; the new page starts as it has gone, so the two never lie over each other, and its footer band follows it a `micro` later, rising 24. The rail's rows change at once; the wire's lit length grows to the new current symbol (or retracts to it) over `long` on `emphasized_decel`, its end on whole device pixels, and each symbol it reaches lights over `short` as the light gets there (the new current one from `medium` in), one it leaves going out as the light leaves it. Reduce motion makes the page a fade, the page left going at once, and the wire and symbols change at once.

**In any order** (first boot): every step takes a press, whatever has been reached, and each step shows its own standing rather than its place in the flow: its check once done, a reboot's symbol in `primary`, Waiting and Not here as their muted symbols (`Step`'s mark), its symbol humming while its work runs, `error` once it failed. The current step is still the quiet fill, and only that: the wire's light follows what is done, not where the reader stands, each length lit where both its steps are done, so a run of two or more done steps in a row is lit along its whole, so it never moves as the reader goes from page to page; a step that joins a run lights its stretch over `short`. Going on or back passes by a step that isn't on this machine, though a press still opens it. The band then has a `Text` Skip before the `Filled` button, which does the page's own work (Enroll a finger, with its symbol before its words) and stays on the page while it runs: the button keeps its room, unseen, with a `Spinner` and the work's words (Running…) in its place, so nothing beside it moves and nothing is marked wrong; Enter waits for the work. Once it is done the button is Continue. A page's work moving on redraws its body in place and its step with it, with no move of the template's own. A window drawn as glass (a first-boot card opened alone from the Command menu) shows the glass under the rail and the page, not a surface of its own; first boot itself is Kōbō's whole screen, on glass as the installer's questions are.

**Pages kept.** A page shown once stays drawn, hidden and out of the keyboard's reach, so going back to it only shows it again, with its footer worked out anew; the page before the current one and the first done step are drawn in idle time with the rail as it will stand there, and in a live flow (first boot's, `drawsAhead`) the page after it too, so a press on any of them draws nothing in the press. A page whose body reads the whole flow (Kōbō's Last look, the install) is drawn each time it shows.

**Its host's ends.** Back or Esc on the first page and Continue or Enter on the last are its host's to take: in Kōbō the first goes back to the welcome, and the last page's own action (the held install) hands over to the install's scene. A page may go back inside itself (`back`): Back and Esc then go back its own step (Make it yours's [Stepper](stepper.md)), and only from its first leave the page. A flow opened on one page alone (a first-boot card from the Command menu) has nothing before it: its Back reads Close (`Text`, `close`), and Close or Esc closes its host.

**Answers that change.** A page's blocker, its note and Continue's words follow its answers as they are typed: the band is drawn again in place, with no motion, the keyboard staying on Continue if it was there.

**The battery** at the rail's foot shows how full it is (`battery_full`, then three, two and one bar, or `battery_charging_full` on the charger) beside its percent in `label`, both `on_surface_variant`; at 20% or under on battery, both `warning`.

**Keys:** Enter is the page's `Filled` action; Esc goes back a page; ↑ ↓ (k j) move between the rail's pressable steps and Space opens one; Tab goes rail, page, Back, Skip, Continue. A page arriving puts the keyboard on its first thing to do, or where the page says it lands (Kōbō's Last look, on the hold). A page's body can go on as Continue does: Enter on the row of its list that is already its answer goes on, where the first Enter on another row picks it.

*Not built yet: neither library has the template; Kōbō draws its rail, panel and footer by hand (`crates/kubaos-kobo/src/view.rs`), and the updater doesn't exist.*
