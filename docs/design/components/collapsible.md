## `Collapsible`

Rust `collapsible::collapsible`; QML `Collapsible`. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, Collapsible, in `design/mockups/`.

A titled part that opens to show more: a header as tall as a row (44, `size.row`) at the control radius, `space.s12` inside: a chevron (`expand_more`, 20) at its start in `on_surface_variant`, then, `space.s12` on, its title in `body_strong` (`on_surface`, one line, an ellipsis where it doesn't fit), and at its end an optional summary, `space.s12` from the title, in `label`, `on_surface_variant`, one line up to half the header's width with an ellipsis ("12 · 4.1 GB", "3 devices"). The summary stays while the part is open; with none, the end is empty. Open, the content sits `space.s8` under the header and `space.s12` in at the end, and at the start in line with the title, past the chevron, so the content brings no inset of its own. The header has no fill and never sticks. The system monitor's process details.

**On a page.** Where a Collapsible sits among a page's own fields (Kōbō's username and machine name, its Git identity), its header hangs its `space.s12` past the content's edges, as a [button bar](button-bar.md)'s Text button hangs its padding: the chevron is on the content's start edge, in line with the fields above and below, the summary ends on the end edge, and the lit tube reaches into the page's padding. In a card, whose 16 would leave the header 4 from its edge, it doesn't hang.

**Opening.** The room under the header eases from nothing to the content's height over `medium` on `standard_decel` and back over `short` on `standard_accel`, cutting the content and the rail while it moves and nothing once it is open, so a lit control inside keeps its glow. The chevron points right while it is closed and turns a quarter to point down over the same time and curve. The header never moves. With reduce motion the room opens and shuts at once, the chevron jumps, and the content fades in over `short`.

**The rail.** Open, an unlit rail of neon runs down the content's start side, centred under the chevron's centre, from the content's top to its foot inside its padding, square at both ends: the tone at the spinner's ring's `opacity.spinner_track`, as thick as the tabs' rail (`tabs.rail`, 2 at night; `tabs.rail_day`, 3 by day), whole device pixels. Under the pointer the strip takes the state layer, a faint pill (`on_surface` at `opacity.hover`), and the line rests; pinned lit it fades over `short` to the core (`primary_core`) with the content glow round it at night, the tone by day, solid glass (`primary_glass`) with the signs off. A hint, "Close" and the title, in the hint's look, sits `space.s8` past the rail's strip at the pointer's height, kept within the rail's length, and follows the pointer down it. The strip that takes the pointer is `size.collapsible_rail` (12) wide, centred on the line. A press anywhere on it closes the part; where the header has scrolled out of its scroll view, the view scrolls it back in at its nearest edge, smoothly unless reduce motion is on. Pressed, the strip's layer goes to `opacity.press`. It is no stop for Tab. Disabled, the rail is at `opacity.disabled_content` and takes no pointer.

**States.** The header follows the neon as a Text button does: hovered and pressed, the state layer (`on_surface` at `opacity.hover`, then `opacity.press`), with no line and no glow; lit (the keyboard on it), the tube inside its edge, `primary`, with the glow outside at night, and a key's press runs it white-hot; signs off, the tube is `primary_glass` with no glow. Disabled (no `on_toggle`, `enabled: false`): its title, chevron and summary at `opacity.disabled_content`, no press, and no stop for Tab. What it holds is the owner's to dim with it, and in iced to disable; in QML it takes no input with its header, since Qt's `enabled` passes down.

**Keys.** Enter or Space on the header toggles it, once per press; Tab reaches the content's controls only while it is open. The keyboard stays on the header; the rail is the pointer's alone.

*Not built yet: both libraries put the chevron at the header's end and swap its glyph instead of turning it, light the header with a state layer, have no summary and no rail, and leave the reveal to the shell (`kubaos-ui` draws it open or shut); the mockup draws the rule.*
