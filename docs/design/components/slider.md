# Slider

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `Slider`

Rust `slider::slider`; QML `Slider`.

A value from 0 to 1, or one of a few stops (`stepped`), as a pill, full width: regular (`slider`, 28) or compact (`slider_compact`, 24, for a dense panel: the control center, the system monitor). The fill is the value: its round end marks it, the value being that end circle's centre, so at 0 the fill is the circle behind the icon and at 1 it reaches the far end; the pointer maps to that centre. The icon (`label`'s 16) sits in that circle, always on the fill, in its content colour (`on_primary`). The value sits at the far end, 12 in: a percentage in `label_number`, or a name in `label` (a stop's, "Muted", "Auto · 55%"), drawn in `on_surface` over the track and the fill's content colour over the fill, split where the fill ends. A compact slider shows a number only under the pointer, while held or focused, fading over `micro`; a name always shows. Steps show as dots (6) on both sides of the fill: those the fill has passed in its content colour at `subdued`, those ahead in `on_surface_variant`. The mark the value rests on (a step at rest, or a sticky point while the value holds there) turns solid, 10 regular and 8 compact, in the fill's content colour, popping in over `short` on `pop`; while a drag moves between marks there is none. No dot is drawn under the icon or the label, with 4 clear of each: they mark that place already. A drag follows the pointer; on a slider with stops it moves freely and settles on the nearest stop when released, over `short` on `standard_decel`. A sticky point (an option, for a surface that needs one: a volume boost to 150% holding at 100%) holds a drag within 12 (`slider_hold`) of it. Left and Right step by a twentieth or a stop, Home and End go to the ends, the wheel steps. The icon is a button where the slider has a toggle: volume's mutes and unmutes, keeping the level to come back to; brightness's switches Auto, where a light sensor and a service driving the backlight from it exist, its fill then the sensor's level in the softer, desaturated accent (`secondary`, its content `on_secondary`). On a slider with stops each press steps one stop from high to low and back, turning at either end. It takes effect as it moves, so it carries a live value. The fill lights up: under the pointer it brightens by `press`, and while it is pressed or dragged by `held`, toward the palette's bright end (`on_surface` in dark, `surface` in light). Its focus tube is a pill on its box's edge.

**States:** resting, hovered, held, focused and disabled (no action, `enabled: false`); stepped, the mark the value rests on solid.
