# Slider

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `Slider`

Rust `slider::slider`; QML `Slider`.

A value from 0 to 1, or one of a few stops (`stepped`), as a pill, full width: regular (`slider`, 28) or compact (`slider_compact`, 24, for a dense panel: the control center, the system monitor). The fill is the value: its round end marks it, the value being that end circle's centre, so at 0 the fill is the circle behind the icon and at 1 it reaches the far end; the pointer maps to that centre. The icon (`label`'s 16) sits in that circle, always on the fill, in its content colour (`on_primary`, or `on_secondary` toggled). The value sits at the far end, 12 in: a percentage in `label_number`, or a name in `label` (a stop's, or a toggled slider's: "Muted", "Auto · 55%"), drawn in `on_surface` over the track and the fill's content colour over the fill, split where the fill ends. A compact slider shows a number only under the pointer, while held or focused, fading over `micro`; a name always shows. Steps show as dots (6) on both sides of the fill: those the fill has passed in its content colour at `subdued`, those ahead in `on_surface_variant`. The mark the value rests on (a step at rest, or a sticky point while the value holds there) turns solid, 10 regular and 8 compact, in the fill's content colour, popping in over `short` on `pop`; while a drag moves between marks there is none. No dot is drawn under the icon or the label, with 4 clear of each: they mark that place already. A drag follows the pointer; on a slider with stops it moves freely and settles on the nearest stop when released, over `short` on `standard_decel`. A sticky point (an option, for a surface that needs one: a volume boost to 150% holding at 100%) holds a drag within 12 (`slider_hold`) of it. Left and Right step by a twentieth or a stop, and Home and End go to the ends. Only the horizontal wheel steps it, a notch at a time, right raising it: a tilted wheel, or the wheel turned with Shift. The vertical wheel scrolls past it and never moves it. It takes effect as it moves, so it carries a live value. The fill lights up: under the pointer it brightens by `press`, and while it is pressed or dragged by `held`, toward the palette's bright end (`on_surface` in dark, `surface` in light). Its focus tube is a pill on its box's edge.

**Toggled.** A continuous slider may have a toggle, which makes its icon a button and gives the slider a second state, Toggled, that a press on the icon turns on and the next press turns off. Volume's icon mutes it, and unmuting returns to the level it had; brightness's switches Auto on, where a light sensor and a service driving the backlight from it exist, and pressing again goes back to manual. Toggled, the fill takes the softer, desaturated accent (`secondary`, its content `on_secondary`), fading between the two tones over `short` on `standard` with the fill where it is; the icon is the toggled one (`volume_off`, the Auto symbol); and the value shows as a name ("Muted", "Auto · 55%"). The fill stays at a level: muted, the one unmuting comes back to (62%, never empty at 0); on Auto, the sensor's live level. Moving the value, by a drag, a key or the horizontal wheel, leaves Toggled from where it is: a drag on a muted volume unmutes it and the value follows the pointer, and on Auto it goes manual at the dragged value. The slider's owner is told each time Toggled turns. A slider with stops has no toggle: each press on its icon steps one stop from high to low and back, turning at either end.

**The icon's button** follows the [icon button](icon-button.md)'s rules. At rest nothing shows it is a button: it is the icon on the fill. Lit under the pointer, it is a Filled icon button in the fill's tone: its circle brightens by `press` toward the palette's bright end, as the fill does, the tube burns inside the circle's edge, with the glow outside at night and the tube without glow by day, and its hint names what a press does ("Mute", "Unmute", "Auto brightness", "Manual brightness", "Step"). Pressed, the circle brightens by `held` and the glow takes `focus.pressed_glow`. While the icon's button is lit, the slider isn't. It is no stop for Tab of its own: the keys move the value.

**States:** resting, hovered, held, focused and disabled (no action, `enabled: false`); stepped, the mark the value rests on solid; toggled, with a toggle, in the softer accent with its value named. The icon's button rests, lights and is pressed on its own.
