# Pill

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `Pill`

QML `Pill`. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, Pill, in `design/mockups/`. *QML only: the bar is the shell's, and no app has one. It is on the QML specimen's "Shell only" page, which the parity check leaves out.*

A group in the bar: a pill a small control tall (`control_sm`, 32), on `surface_container`, its content a row 8 apart (`spacing`) centred on device pixels, with 12 at each end between the curve and the content (`padding`; 8 for a lone symbol, the bell, the proxy, the updates). A pill of its own tone takes `color` and `ink` together: the recording pill's `error_container` with `on_error_container`, the preview pill's `cold` tint with `cold`, the sharing pill's `sharing_container` with `on_sharing_container`, and the camera pill's `error_container` with `on_error_container`, as the recording pill's, so the two read as equals. Past one share or camera, the pill shows the count `space.s4` after its symbol; its ends stay `space.s8`, counted or not, so as the count comes and goes the symbol stays solid and only the count fades. *Not built yet: the sharing and camera tones are in the mockups only.*

A symbol and its own value (a system monitor reading, the battery's share, the recording's time, a scheduled action's time left) are one thing in that row: 4 apart (`space.s4`), the value straight after its symbol, so each pair reads as one beside the 8 between a pill's things. Where a value is held to a width so its neighbours stay put as it changes (a reading as wide as "100%"), the room it doesn't use falls after it, never between it and its symbol.

`interactive` makes it take the pointer, lit by the pointer alone: its tube inside its edge in its tone (the accent, the recording pill's error, the sharing pill's `sharing`, the camera pill's `recording`) with the glow outside at night; pressed, the tube white-hot and the glow brighter, by day its `ink` tinting it (`opacity.press_tint`). It takes a click (`clicked`), a right click (`rightClicked`) and the wheel (`scrolled`, a step up or down per notch). It is never a stop for Tab and draws no focus tube, so a panel's keyboard focus never wanders into the bar; the bar's own actions are Items in the Command menu too, so none needs the bar's keyboard focus. It takes its own pointer rather than a `Pressable`'s, which only clicks: the bar's pills right-click and scroll too.

On an `interactive` pill, `tooltip` shows in a `Hint` under it while the pointer is on it, moved sideways as far as keeps it whole inside the window, 8 from its edge, and kept there as its words change (the status pill's).

When its content changes, what is in both the old and the new content (the same part, or an equal one, such as the recording's dot beside its new time) stays solid and moves with the change, and inside a symbol and its value only the part that differs changes; what leaves fades out as its room closes, and what arrives fades in as its room opens, each room as wide as its content times how open it is (rounded up to a device pixel) and the gap beside it closing with it, over `medium` on `standard_decel`, as a list reorders. The pill's width eases with those rooms, so nothing draws over its neighbours: new words replacing old (a keyboard layout switched) cross-fade as one room closes and the other opens; now playing's song fades as it folds to its controls. Only a leaving or arriving room cuts its content, and only while it moves: at rest nothing is cut, so the hint of a press target inside a pill (an audio pill's circle) floats whole under it. Reduce motion changes the content at once, with no fade and no ease. Content that animates its own width every frame isn't put in a pill, whose ease would trail behind it: the workspaces draw a pill of their own.

It isn't a `Chip` (a fact or a pick inside a surface, 24 tall) or a `Badge` (a count or a dot on a symbol's corner): it is the bar's own group, as tall as the bar's controls.

**States:** rest; an interactive one lit and pressed, its tooltip in a hint while the pointer is on it. It is never focused: no Tab stop in the bar.

*Not built yet: `Pill` lays the state layer of its `ink` over its fill while hovered and pressed. The system monitor's reading sits at the right of its held box (`SysmonPill.qml`), so its room falls between it and its symbol, and spaces its readings 12 apart; the recording's dot and time are 8 apart (`RecordingIndicator.qml`).*
