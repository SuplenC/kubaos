## `Backdrop`

QML `Backdrop` (polkit's prompt) and Rust `layout::backdrop` (Kōbō's welcome, its questions and its done screen), a template. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Templates, Backdrop, in `design/mockups/`. The lock screen is a template of its own, [Lock](lock.md). *Not built yet: polkit (`modules/polkit/Polkit.qml`) and Kōbō (`crates/kubaos-kobo/src/view.rs`) each draw their own picture, scrim and layout.*

A full-screen moment: a picture, a scrim over it, and one thing in its middle, centred on whole device pixels with the odd one after it.

- **The picture** is Kōbō's wallpaper; polkit lies over the desktop it asks on and has none of its own. Before it has arrived the screen is `surface_container_lowest`, and it fades in over `slow`.
- **The scrim** is one of three. Behind words (Kōbō's welcome and done): a band of `surface` at `opacity.backdrop` across the screen, solid from `space.s64` above the thing to `space.s64` below it and fading to nothing over those `space.s64`, so every text role keeps 4.5:1 over any picture. Behind a dialog or a panel (polkit): `scrim` at `opacity.scrim` over all of it. Behind a flow of questions that is the whole screen (Kōbō's): glass, the picture's blurred copy under `surface` at `opacity.glass` over all of it, so the street is still there, out of focus, with no panel and no edge.
- **The thing**: polkit's `Dialog`, its `Field` led by `key` and, where a security key with a fingerprint sensor answers polkit, ended by `fingerprint` in `primary`, the lock's reader mark ([Lock](lock.md)); with no such key the field has none. A finger the key doesn't know turns the mark `error` and the field's placeholder to Finger not recognised, and nothing shakes; nothing changes while it reads, and a known finger authenticates. PAM's cues (Touch the fingerprint sensor, Touch the key to confirm the PIN) are the note under the field, Authenticate disabled while PAM asks for nothing. *Not built yet: `Polkit.qml`'s field has no reader mark.* Kōbō's welcome, install or done column, `size.column` wide, its words centred; or, on glass, Kōbō's questions (StepRailPage), `space.s32` in from the screen's edges (`space.s16` under two columns' width), no wider than a rail and two columns with `space.s40` beside each, centred, their words where their page puts them. A glass panel (`glassPanel`: its blurred copy of the picture under `surface` at `opacity.glass`, a soft hairline inside its edge, at the surface radius, casting `shadow.panel`) is the window a first-boot card opened alone from the Command menu is drawn in.
- **The picture's light and place.** The picture can come toward you, growing to `scale.push` round the middle of its bottom edge, and can go dark toward `surface_container_lowest`, a layer over it at any share from nothing to all of it. Kōbō's room moves both: its welcome lights up from black and settles back from the push; its install starts at `opacity.scrim`'s dark and lifts it as the work runs; its done screen goes to black as the mark is written in, then lights up and comes toward you as the mark strikes; Reboot takes it to black.
- **Two at once.** Where one backdrop hands over to another (Kōbō's welcome to its questions, where the street goes out of focus as the sharp picture fades off the blurred one, its install to its done screen), the upper draws no base colour of its own, so as its picture fades the one under it shows rather than `surface_container_lowest`.
- **A short room:** where the screen is shorter than the thing and `space.s32` above and below it, the parts marked as giving way go first (Kōbō's sign), so the words and the action always show.

Motion: it arrives over `scene` on `emphasized_decel`, the picture and scrim fading in and the thing fading in as it rises `space.s24` into place, and lifts over `scene` on `emphasized_accel`, fading off what is under it while the thing rises `space.s24` on. A prompt over the desktop arrives as a `Dialog` does, over `long`. With reduce motion each fades, capped at `short`, with no rise.

Keys: Enter answers or takes the screen's `Filled` action; Esc is polkit's Cancel and does nothing on Kōbō's welcome and done; Tab goes round the thing's controls and never leaves it. Arriving lands on polkit's field or Kōbō's action; lifting gives the keyboard back to what had it.
