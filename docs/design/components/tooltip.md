# Tooltip

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `Tooltip`

Rust `tooltip::tooltip`, `tooltip::with_tooltip`; QML `Tooltip`.

- **A `Tooltip` explains a control** where a `Hint` only names it: a title in `body_strong`, the body in `small` and `on_surface_variant` 4 under it, and what it holds 8 below (the keys it is about, as `KeyHints`), on a `Popover` as wide as a menu (240). It shows while the pointer is over its control or the keyboard is on it, 8 above the control and starting where it starts, held inside the window: above, since the controls it explains sit in a toolbar at a panel's foot, and from the control's start, since a card centred on a chip at a toolbar's start would hang past the panel. Where there is no room above (a button in a header at the window's top, the notifications panel's), it opens 8 below instead, still from the control's start. In QML it may be held inside a panel rather than the window (`within`), so a card from a button in the panel's corner stays on the panel. Like the hint it floats over the window, no part of the page's layout (the mockups' `floatingTooltip`, which anything that needs a tooltip uses): the control keeps its own place and nothing round it moves. Near a panel's end it lines up with the control's end instead of its start, so it stays on the panel, as Do not disturb's in the notifications panel's header and the keys button's in its corner do; and in QML it shows only while its control shows whole, never for one a scroll has cut. It is for a control whose words can't say enough on their own (the keyboard section's Mac accents chip), never for a sentence the page should show.
- **A name and its key** (`keyTooltip` in the mockups): for a control its name already says enough about, whose key a surface doesn't show beside it (the wallpaper picker's bar), the card holds the name in `body_strong` and the key's keycaps 12 after it, on one line, 4 apart, as wide as they are and never wider than a menu. It shows and hides as every tooltip does. *Not built yet: neither library has it.*
- **A term in a sentence** (`term` and `termed` in the mockups): a word the sentence can't stop to explain takes a card too. The word stays in its line, dotted under (`text-decoration: underline dotted`, `stroke.thin` below the words) with the help pointer, and Tab reaches it, lit by the cold focus tube; its card floats 8 above it, from its start, while the pointer or the keyboard is on it. The card sits beside the sentence in the layout, tied to the word by an anchor, so the sentence holds only its own words and wraps as any does. It is the fine print's joke on first boot's Kotodama card; a word the reader needs is written out instead. *Not built yet: neither library has it.*
- **Answering a press** (`floatingTooltip`'s `shows: "pressed"`): the card shows when its control is pressed, not while it is lit, stays for two holds (`duration.hold`) and fades, and a press while it shows starts it again; the control does nothing else it doesn't already do. For a button whose answer is a word, as Copy's "Copied", and the Kotodama card's fake ad's button. *Not built yet: neither library has it.*
- **The card** is a `Popover`: `surface_container` (`surface_container_high` at night), raised to the ladder's step 4 with its shadow and the rim, the control radius (12), 12 inside. The title and the body break between words in its width. A control it explains shows no `Hint`.
- **It isn't neon:** no line, no glow and no states of its own. It fades in over `short` (`standard`) as its control lights up and out over `short` as the control goes back to rest, and never flickers, even as a surface appearing: it is a hint-like popup, not a light. A power change only recolours it. Over a disabled control it still shows under the pointer, as a `Hint` does. *Not built yet: the libraries show it over a disabled control only when the app asks.*
