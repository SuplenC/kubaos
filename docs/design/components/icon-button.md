# IconButton and Hint

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `IconButton`

Rust `button::icon_button(glyph, label)`; QML `IconButton`.

**Kinds:** `Filled`, `Tonal`, `Text`, `Danger`, `Success` and `Warning`, each with its [Button](button.md) kind's colours: at night Filled rests on the unlit fill (`primary_fill`) with an `on_primary_fill` symbol, Tonal on `secondary_container` with a dimmed symbol, Text with no fill and its symbol the tone unlit (`primary_rest`), and Danger, Success and Warning burn on `burn_error`, `burn_success` and `burn_warning`, always lit, with a dark symbol (`on_burn_error` and the rest).

**States** are the button's (`docs/DESIGN.md`, "States"), in every kind and size, with no state layer:

- **Rest:** unlit at night, as above.
- **Lit:** hovered or focused from the keyboard, one state, and its hint shows. Signs on, at night while the lights burn, the line inside the circle's edge burns (the core, `stroke.thin` wide, on Filled and the burning kinds; the tube, `primary` at `focus.tube`, on Tonal and Text) with the glow outside it, the only thing past the circle, and the symbol brightens as its kind's label does: Filled's with a tiny glow, Text's burning as the core with the content glow. Signs off, by day always and at night while a power change has the lights out, the same line is glass (`primary_fill_glass`, `primary_glass`, `burn_error_glass`, `burn_success_glass`, `burn_warning_glass`) with no glow and the symbol at rest.
- **Pressed:** overdriven and lit: Filled's and Tonal's fill lifts 0.035 at night (0.04 darker by day), Text's tube runs to the core (by day the tone under it at `opacity.press_tint`), the burning kinds' lit fill goes 8% further toward white (0.06 darker than rest by day); the glow is `focus.pressed_glow` times the lit one.
- **Disabled:** grey, with no tone, no line and no glow; no action and no focus. Its hint still shows while the pointer is over it.

**Motion.** Lighting up, going back to rest and pressing are fades (`short`, and `micro` for the press); the hint fades in and out over `short`. The flicker is for power changes only: in a theme change and dark ⇄ light a lit icon button flickers to its signs-off look and back, and one at rest just recolours.

*Not built yet: the libraries draw hover and press as state layers, strike focus, light `Danger` in the accent on `error`, have no `Success` or `Warning` kind and round the hint at the small radius (4); the mockups' IconButton page (`design/mockups/src/pages/atoms/icon-button.ts`) draws the rule.*

## `Hint`

Rust `hint::hint`, `hint::hint_at_start`; QML `Hint`, `held: "start"`.

- **`IconButton` is a circle as tall as the label button of its size** (32, 40, 48), in all six kinds. **Its label is a required argument.** It is the accessible name, and shows in a `Hint` while the pointer is over it or the keyboard is on it: a bubble in the inverse colours, `label` type, padding 8 × 4, the row radius (8), centred under the control, 8 below it. Held to its start (`start`; `hint_at_start` in Rust, `held: "start"` in QML), its left edge lines up with the control's: a slider's icon, so a long name never hangs past the slider's edge, or a counter's name, which a double-click renames, so its hint reads from the name's first letter rather than under the middle of a name that may run half the row. Held at the window's end (`end`; `hintAtEnd` in the mockups' `IconButton`, a page title's reset), it moves aside only as far as it must to stay inside the window. Any bubble that centring would run past a side of the box that cuts its control (a pane that scrolls, the window) moves along that side until it is whole inside, 8 from the edge, so its words are never cut; in a [Scroll](scroll.md) that edge is the scroll's own box, not the glow's reach past it, which the part beside it covers. *Not built yet: neither library moves it.* **It opens above** its control, 8 above it and placed across it as below, where a part asks for it (a toolbar at a well's foot, the keyboard layouts', `hintAbove` in the mockups' `IconButton`) or where the box that cuts or scrolls its control has no room for it below, so a bubble never makes a well scroll or hangs past what holds it. *Not built yet: neither library opens it above; QML's floats over the window, 8 below its control, so a well never scrolls for it, but at a well's foot it covers what is under the well.* In QML the bubble floats over the window, but only while its control shows whole: a control a scroll has cut, or scrolled out of view, takes its hint with it, so it never floats over what the scroll shows instead. An icon alone says nothing out loud, so an icon button can't be built without its label.
- **A hint isn't neon:** no line and no glow, the same bubble by night and by day. It shows while its control is lit (hovered or focused) and fades in and out with it over `short` on `standard`; it never flickers, even in a power change, where it just recolours. The bubble is made the first time its control is pointed at or reached by the keyboard, not with the control, and it starts hidden so its first show fades in on the same frame as any later one. Over a disabled control it still shows while the pointer is there. The mockups' Hint page (`design/mockups/src/pages/atoms/hint.ts`) shows it.
- **A symbol may morph between two states** (`morph` in the mockups' `IconButton`, `morphIcon` and `setMorph`): play and pause, where a press swaps one for the other. The symbol is two solid shapes on the symbols' 24-unit grid, a stroke's width out from the outline symbols' paths so it stands as big; every corner moves from one state's place to the other's as `--morph` goes 0 to 1 over `short` on `standard`. Play's triangle is its two halves, which part into pause's two bars; never a cross-fade. With reduce motion it changes at once. A lit Filled button's glow follows the shapes where they stand. The owner names the button for what a press does. The media player's play button is one. *Not built yet: neither library morphs a symbol; only the mockups do.*
- **A held icon button** (`hold` in the mockups' `IconButton`) acts only once held for `duration.hold` (1100), with the pointer or with Enter or Space, never on a press: the reset beside a Settings row, a sub-section's and a page's. While it is held, the Spinner's ring is drawn round its circle in place of its lit tube, whose line and glow give way, `stroke.thin` in whole device pixels inside its edge: the unlit ring at `opacity.spinner_track` and the lit arc running clockwise from the top as the hold fills, both `primary` on a Text or Tonal one, its core and glow at night as the Spinner's are, and on a filled kind its label's colour (`on_burn_error` on a Danger one), which keeps its contrast on the fill. Letting go early, the pointer leaving or the keyboard going elsewhere lets it go: the ring is gone at once and the hold runs back over `short`; at rest the ring isn't drawn, so a Text one stays quiet. Its timing, its keys and what lets it go are the one hold a held `Button` and a held menu item share; the speed setting divides it. *Not built yet: neither library has a held icon button.*
- A button's icon is its label's style's (see [DESIGN.md, "Icons by type"](../../DESIGN.md#icons-by-type)); an icon button takes the icon of the label button of its size.
- **An icon button a `Tooltip` explains shows the tooltip instead of its hint** (`IconButton::explained`, `explained` in QML): the card already names it, and a bubble under it as well would say the name twice, below a control that may sit at the screen's foot. The notifications panel's Do not disturb and keys buttons are two. **Every other icon button shows its hint**, however obvious its symbol: the panel's Clear all and a notification card's dismiss among them.
