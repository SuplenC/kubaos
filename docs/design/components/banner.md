## `Banner`

Rust `banner::banner`; QML `Banner`. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, Banner, in `design/mockups/`.

A message that matters on the page itself, with what to do about it inside: a status message lying flat in the page as a card does, one container role above what it sits on (`surface_container` on a window's `surface`), with the sunken soft hairline, the content radius (16), 16 inside. Every tone is that same flat box; its tone is a rail of neon down its start side, from the top padding to the bottom one with round ends: the tabs' and the collapsible's rail made thicker, `banner.rail` (4) at night and `banner.rail_day` (4) by day in whole device pixels, filling a lane 4 wide 12 in from the box's edge, so the words start 12 past the lane, 28 in. At night the rail is the tone's core with the content glow round it while the signs are on, and glass while they are off; by day it is the tone with no glow. In it a 24 symbol in the tone on the title's first line, 16 from a `subheading` title in `on_surface` over a `body` line and any paragraphs more in `on_surface_variant`, 4 apart, all wrapping; its actions a [ButtonBar](button-bar.md) 16 under the words, across their width, with the bar's rules (Back at its start, the action it is about at its end); an optional foot 16 under the rest (a held button's `timed_bar`, filling over `duration.hold`). Tones: `Info` (its rail and symbol `primary`), `Warning` (`warning`) and `Danger` (`error`). On a `Danger` banner the loudest thing is the `Danger` button that does the damage, held, with the danger bar along the foot, so it can't be pressed by accident. An empty body leaves the title alone, a line tall. It isn't raised, never goes by itself, has no close and isn't a stop for Tab; its actions are, in reading order. Kōbō's Last chance is a `Danger` banner holding Back and the held Install.

*Not built yet: both libraries draw it at the surface radius (24) with a hairline in its tone and no rail, `Danger` on `error_container`, and its actions beside its words.*
