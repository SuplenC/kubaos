# Banner

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `Banner`

Rust `banner::banner`; QML `Banner`. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, Banner, in `design/mockups/`.

A message that matters on the page itself, with what to do about it inside: a status message lying flat in the page as a card does, one container role above what it sits on (`surface_container` on a window's `surface`), with the sunken soft hairline, the content radius (16), 16 inside. Every tone is that same flat box; its tone is a rail of neon down its start side, from the top padding to the bottom one with round ends: the tabs' and the collapsible's rail made thicker, `banner.rail` (4) at night and `banner.rail_day` (4) by day in whole device pixels, filling a lane 4 wide 12 in from the box's edge, so the words start 12 past the lane, 28 in. At night the rail is the tone's core with a lit control's glow round it, near and far, while the signs are on, and glass while they are off; by day it is the tone with no glow. In it a 24 symbol in the tone on the title's first line, 16 from a `subheading` title in `on_surface` over a `body` line and any paragraphs more in `on_surface_variant`, 4 apart, all wrapping; its actions a [ButtonBar](button-bar.md) 16 under the words, across their width, with the bar's rules (Back at its start, the action it is about at its end); an optional foot 16 under the rest (a held button's `timed_bar`, filling over `duration.hold`). Tones: `Info` (its rail and symbol `primary`), `Warning` (`warning`), `Danger` (`error`) and `Tip` (`cold`). Any line names keys as it goes, each key a [Keycap](keycap.md) standing in the line, its middle on the middle of the words' capitals, the lines then a keycap and 4 apart (24) so they stay even. On a `Danger` banner the loudest thing is the `Danger` button that does the damage, held, with the danger bar along the foot, so it can't be pressed by accident. An empty body leaves the title alone, a line tall. It isn't raised, never goes by itself, has no close and isn't a stop for Tab; its actions are, in reading order. Kōbō's Last chance is a `Danger` banner holding Back and the held Install.

**Small** is the size that stands outside a card, under a section's heading or alone on a page: the same flat box, hairline and rail, 12 inside, the rail's lane 8 in and the words 8 past it, 20 in, at the content radius (16) of the cards beside it; its rail the tabs' own, `tabs.rail` (2) at night and `tabs.rail_day` (3) by day, a banner's thinned a step for a box this size. In it the symbol at `label`'s 16 beside a `label` title in the tone, 8 apart, and under them, 4 apart and starting where the symbol does, its lines in `small` in `on_surface_variant`, wrapping. **Line** is the size that lives inside a card, under the row it is about: no fill, no hairline and no title, only the rail, as thin as a Small one's, down the start of its words from the top of its first line to the foot of its last, and the words 8 past the rail's lane 4 wide, in `small` in `on_surface_variant`. It may lead with its symbol at `small`'s 16 in the tone, 8 before the words, the lines it wraps to starting where the words do. Every line of it is a keycap and 4 tall (24), whether it names a key or not, and the symbol and each key stand with their middle on the middle of the words' capitals. **A tip** is a `Tip` banner teaching a faster or hidden way to do something, so it is no secret: one whole sentence naming its keys where they fall ("Hold Super and drag with the right button to resize a window from anywhere on it; the left button moves it."); a tip taught through the Command menu always reads "Press Super Space and type “…” to …". Outside a card it is Small and titled, the `lightbulb` and "Tip" in `cold` over the sentence: under a section's heading, the first of its content, about the whole part, or alone on a page. Inside a card it is a Line under its setting, across the row inside its padding, led by the `lightbulb` (`TIP_SYMBOL` in the mockups' `lib/banner.ts` turns the symbol off). It has no actions, no close and no stop for Tab, and a press on it does nothing ([list.md](list.md), [layout.md](layout.md)).

*Not built yet: both libraries draw it at the surface radius (24) with a hairline in its tone and no rail, `Danger` on `error_container`, and its actions beside its words.*
