# ColourPicker

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `ColourPicker`

Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, ColourPicker, in `design/mockups/`.

A colour picked for something drawn over a picture: Auto, which leaves the colour to its user (the lock clock takes the picture's own tint under its digits), one of the scheme's roles as a swatch, or Custom, a colour of its own picked by its hue and its tone. It is three controls, each the control of one row of its form, which labels them: the choice, the hue track and the Tone slider. The wallpaper picker's clock editor sets the lock clock's colour with it, offering Auto, `primary`, `secondary`, `cold` and `on_surface` (named Text), and Custom. Its words are options with defaults: Auto's label (`autoLabel`, "Auto"), the Custom swatch's name (`customLabel`, "Custom") and what the hue track reads out as (`hueLabel`, "Hue").

- **The choice** is a row of picks, `space.s8` apart, inside a pill (`radius.full`) that is the focus tube's box, padded `space.s4` at its top and foot and `space.s4` plus twice `stroke.thin` at its ends, the reach of the picked ring and its gap past the swatch, so a ring at either end of a line clears the tube by `space.s4`, as it clears the swatch beside it; it wraps when its row is narrower. Auto comes first, a pressable `Chip`, `Selected` while picked and `Neutral` otherwise. A swatch is a circle `size.chip` (24) across in its role's colour, with a hairline rim of `on_surface` at `elevation.rim`; the one picked has a ring of `primary`, `stroke.thin` wide, cut from it by a `stroke.thin` gap in the colour of the surface the picker sits on. That surface is `--k-colour-surface`, which the surface round the picker sets to its own colour role, and which is `surface_container_high` when nothing sets it, the well its clock-editor row sits in. Custom is the last swatch: every hue round a wheel at one light tone (OKLCH 0.75, chroma 0.13), with the custom colour in its middle once it is one, a quarter of the swatch in from its edge, ringed by the same gap.
- **The hue track** is a pill `size.slider_compact` (24) tall, every hue across it at that same tone, a knob `size.slider_stop` (6) across with a `stroke.thin` ring of `surface` round the hue picked, and the hue in degrees in `label_number` at the far end, `space.s12` in, dark in both modes (`surface` by night, `on_surface` by day). Its focus tube is a pill on its box's edge.
- **Tone** is a compact `Slider` with the `brightness_medium` symbol, from OKLCH lightness 0.30 to 0.97, its value a percentage of lightness; a custom colour's chroma is 0.12 at every hue and tone, so every hue stays inside sRGB at the light tones a colour over a picture takes. Before a custom colour is picked, the two start at a light blue (hue 250, lightness 0.85).

**Keys.** The choice is one stop for Tab: Left and Right (h and l) pick the pick before or after, round from either end as `Tabs` do, and the keyboard stays on the choice. Hue and Tone are stops only while Custom is picked: the hue track's Left and Right step a twentieth of the wheel, a degree with Shift, and Home and End go to either end; Tone takes a slider's keys. A click picks; a drag on the hue track follows the pointer.

**States:** rest, the pick ringed (or Auto `Selected`); lit, the choice's or the hue track's focus tube in the accent, with the signs off its glass; under the pointer, a swatch lights as the pressable `Neutral` Auto chip beside it does, its tube (`focus.tube`) inside its edge in the accent with the glow outside at night, and the hue track lights as a `Slider` does, its tube inside its edge and its hues brightened toward the palette's bright end (`on_surface` by night, `surface` by day) by `opacity.press`; pressed, Auto's or a swatch's tube runs to the core with the glow `focus.pressed_glow` times the lit one (by day the accent tints it at `opacity.press_tint`), and the hue track held or dragged brightens its hues by `opacity.held`; lighting fades over `short` and a press over `micro`, both on `standard`; and disabled: every pick at `opacity.disabled_content`, the wheel's chroma lowered by the same share, and the hue track and Tone disabled too, no stop for Tab. Hue and Tone are disabled whenever Custom isn't picked.
