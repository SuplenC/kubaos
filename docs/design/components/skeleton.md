## `Skeleton`

Rust `skeleton::skeleton`; QML `Skeleton`.

The shape of what is loading, held in its place: a `line` of text in a style (as wide as its room, the style's line tall, the small radius), a `circle` an icon size across, or a `block` a size tall at a radius role. It is glass of the accent, `primary` at `opacity.skeleton` (`skeleton_light` by day), with no edge, and it breathes while it shows, as a sign's gas warms up before it lights: every bone's fill rises to `skeleton_peak` (`skeleton_light_peak`) over `duration.hum` and falls back over `hum`, on `standard`, together; at night a faint glow, `skeleton_glow` fading out over `focus.near`, comes and goes with the peak. With reduce motion it holds still at rest. A Wi-Fi or Bluetooth scan with nothing found yet shows skeleton rows, and the system monitor shows skeleton lines for the readings and processes a detailed sample brings until one comes; a `Media` box's placeholder is a block skeleton of its own.
