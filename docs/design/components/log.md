## `Log`

Rust `error::log`; QML `Log`.

The log behind an error or a progress bar, `mono`, newest line at the bottom, opened on demand. The surface radius (24). The caller sets its height as an `Extent`; `log` (232) is the size the specimen and Kōbō use.
