# IconTile

Part of the closed set of components: [DESIGN.md, "Components"](../../DESIGN.md#components) has the rules every component keeps, its variant words and the fixed measures the geometry check holds it to.

## `IconTile`

Rust `icon_tile::icon_tile`, and `icon_tile::initial_tile` for an app that names no symbol; QML `IconTile`, with `icon` empty and the app's `name` for that. Its mockup page is the source of truth: Molecules, IconTile, in `design/mockups/`.

What an app or a sender looks like in a row: a symbol or a picture on a tinted square of one size, sm 32 beside one line (a 20 icon), md 40 beside a title over a subtitle (24, `icon.two_line`) and lg 48 over a `headline` name, the one thing a view is about (32, the headline's: a network or a device being connected), at the control radius, on `surface_container_highest`, which reads on a list, a card and a selected row alike (`error` for a critical notification). On a dialog, whose own fill is `surface_container_highest` at night, the tile steps down to `surface_container` at night and keeps `surface_container_highest` by day, over the dialog's `surface_container_high`, so it shows in both (a dialog's caller line). Its symbol is `on_surface_variant`, or `primary` on the tiles of a list's rows in use (`accent`: the network or the devices connected). An app's icon is measured once when it loads, and the result is kept for that icon: whether it is a plate, and the box it is placed by, so the margin its theme leaves round it drops away. It is a plate when the box of its solid pixels (alpha above `icon_tile.opaque`, a half) is square (short side at least `icon_tile.square`, 0.95, of the long one) and those pixels cover at least `icon_tile.plate`, 85%, of it. A plate is placed by that box, so a soft shadow drawn under it, which reaches further on one side, falls outside the tile and never pushes the plate off its centre; a shape is placed by the box of every pixel it draws (alpha above `icon_tile.ink`). A rounded square covers about 95% and a disc 78.5%, so the line falls in a clear gap. A plate fills the whole tile, centred, and the tile's `radius.control` corners cut it, so the two make one edge. Anything else is a shape, fitted `space.s4` inside the tile, centred: 24 in sm, 32 in md, 40 in lg. Either is kept whole (contain), never cropped and never stretched, its box snapped to whole device pixels and centred as the symbol is, on the nearest device pixel, the odd one before it. A photo (a notification's image) covers the tile. The symbol, or the initial, shows until the picture has loaded and stays when there is none, so every row reads the same size whatever each vendor drew. A count can take the symbol's place (a `NotificationPopup` standing for several from one app): the number in `body_strong`, whose 20 line fills the box the sm tile's 20 symbol does, in the symbol's colour, centred as the symbol is, 99+ past 99. *Not built yet: neither library draws a count on a tile.* The Command menu's rows, the notifications', a `ListRow`'s with `tile` and the control center's connection form. It is a mark, not a control: the row it sits in lights, and the tile doesn't change with it.

An app with no symbol shows its initial instead: the first character of its name, upper-cased (`to_uppercase` of its first grapheme), so a name in another script shows its own (日本語入力 → 日). The letter takes the type style whose `icon` is the tile's symbol box, `body_strong` in sm, `subheading` in md and `headline` in lg, so its line is that box and it sits where the symbol would, in the symbol's colour. It is centred on its ink: down, on the top and bottom of what it draws, so B, L and K sit level and an ideograph takes in what it draws below the baseline; across, on its advance. So a tile shows the picture when there is one, else the symbol the app names, else its initial. Each library measures the ink from the glyph as its own rasterizer draws it at 64 pixels, hinted: iced from cosmic-text's swash, QML from Qt's hinted outline (`Ink`). A kana or a kanji (CJK ideographs through Extension G, U+3134F, and half-width kana) is drawn in M PLUS 1, named rather than left to a fallback that would pick another CJK font a machine has; the mockups' type families end in it for the same reason. A name with nothing in it still makes an `initials` tile, with no letter. The Command menu's rows of desktop apps show it, since an app there names no symbol. Where an app stands as a 16 symbol with no tile under it, a `MenuItem`'s (`menu.md`) or a bar circle's, its initial is drawn by the same rule in `label`, whose symbol is 16, in the symbol's colour.

The iced and QML builds measure each icon once when it loads and cache the result per icon, with the thresholds from the `icon_tile` tokens. *Not built yet:* both still draw an app's icon inset at the symbol's size.
