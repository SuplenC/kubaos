# The media player's film

A cut of a short film for the media player's mockup, so the player has a real video to play, seek and preview. The poster is one frame of the cut, which the player's fixed samples show in place of the playing film.

Changes made: a 36-second cut starting at 0:30 of the original, scaled to 960 × 540, re-encoded to VP9 in WebM, its sound removed; the poster is that cut's frame at 12.27 s, saved as a JPEG.

The player shows the film's title and its maker wherever the film shows.
