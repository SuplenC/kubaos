# The media player's songs

Copies of two tracks for the media player's mockup, so the player has real songs to play and its visualisers real music to read. Each was re-encoded to Opus in Ogg, 48 kHz stereo, from the MP3 downloaded from Pixabay, so it stays small in the repository; nothing else about it was changed.

| File | Title | Artist | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `drum-and-bass-jungle.opus` | Drum and Bass Jungle | Echoes of Lumen | [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/), track 583440 | [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) |
| `into-the-light.opus` | Into the Light | BerryDeep | [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/), track 565406 | [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) |

The player shows each song's title and artist wherever the song shows.
