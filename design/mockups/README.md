# The mockup site

Every UI KubaOS has, as it is meant to be: the single source of truth the builds match. The mockup skill (`.claude/skills/mockup/SKILL.md`) says what a mockup is and what its page must show; this file says how the site works and how a page is added.

## Running it

In `nix develop`, which provides Node, the tokens' generator and Chromium:

| Command | What |
|---|---|
| `just mockups` | Serves the site with hot reload at the address Vite prints. A change to `design/tokens.toml` regenerates `src/tokens.css` and the page follows it. |
| `just mockups-build` | Type-checks and builds it static into `dist/`, then draws each page in headless Chromium to write its moves' recipes into `dist/motion/PAGE.json`. |
| `just mockups-check` | Draws every page at every scale, light and dark, and holds its boxes to the geometry rules and each mocked component to its twin on the specimen's pages (`docs/DESIGN.md`, "The mockups"), then holds every motion player to its recipe (`scripts/player-test.ts`). Part of `just design-check`. |
| `just shots mockups` | Renders the pages' shots, before and after (`docs/agents/design-pr-loop.md`). |

Plain HTML, CSS and TypeScript, built by Vite; no UI framework. The npm dependencies are locked in `package-lock.json`, and `playwright-core` is the version of nixpkgs' Playwright, whose Chromium draws the shots and frames. Use a Chromium-based browser: the icons' stroke uses CSS typed arithmetic.

## The toolbar

Every page has it: light or dark, the theme, the scale (1, 1.25, 1.5, 2), reduce motion, the speed, the flicker of the power changes, the text's length (short, long, a translation 40% longer) and a window size for templates and screens. The page's box is zoomed out by the display's own pixel ratio, so inside it a CSS pixel is a device pixel at every scale: hairlines and the grid land as they do in the builds. Mode, scale and motion apply through CSS custom properties at once; the text's length draws the page again. Picking a theme plays the theme change across the whole page, and picking light or dark plays the dark ⇄ light switch, each from the recipe its player shows (`src/site/sequences.ts`, `src/site/change.ts`); with reduce motion on, the colours swap at once. The themes (`src/site/themes.ts`) are the tokens' own palette and three others matugen made from seed colours, so no page is judged in one accent alone. Each setting is kept in the address (`?mode=light&scale=1.5`) and for the next visit.

A CSS pixel is a device pixel only at a whole pixel ratio: Chromium lays a page out in steps that don't divide a fractional one. On a screen at 1.25, the toolbar says so and names the browser zoom that makes the ratio whole (80%). The shots and `just mockups-check` draw at a ratio of 1.

## Where things are

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `src/tokens.css` | Generated by `kubaos-tokens` (ignored by git): every token as a custom property. A length is its logical pixels times `--px`, recomputed in every `.mock` box. What the tokens say in words, where each colour role comes from and the contrast pairs are strings on `:root`. |
| `src/lib/` | The mocked components and their parts (`text`, `icon`, `row`, `button`, `icon-button`, `hint`, `field`, `keycap`, `tooltip`, `tile`, the focus ring), each the twin of `kubaos-ui`'s and carrying the specimens' probe; `control.ts` and `.k-control` in `lib.css`, the states every control shares. |
| `src/site/page.ts` | The page API: what a page says. |
| `src/site/recipe.ts` | A move's recipe: its tracks on the player's loop as the keyframes it plays, and as the JSON a library reads. |
| `src/site/recolour.ts` | Every colour role moving from one palette to another along a typed OKLCH path (through grey, dawn), as CSS formulas of one animated number. |
| `src/site/moves.ts` | The moves every control makes: lighting up, pressed, and the power changes played on its lit samples. |
| `src/site/sequences.ts`, `src/site/change.ts` | The theme change and the dark ⇄ light switch as moves, and their run across the page. |
| `src/site/themes.ts` | The themes the toolbar paints in. |
| `src/site/contrast.ts` | The contrast pairs and their ratios in any palette, by `kubaos-design contrast`'s arithmetic. |
| `src/site/parts/` | The parts every page is drawn with: the state matrix, the motion player, the keyboard panel, the measures overlay, the description block; and a foundation's shown parts, its painted boxes, swatches and tables. |
| `src/pages/LEVEL/PAGE.ts` | One page each; `src/pages/index.ts` lists them. |
| `scripts/render.ts` | Draws the built site in headless Chromium, as shots and geometry frames, or as each page's motion recipes (`--motion`). |
| `scripts/player-test.ts` | Seeks every motion player on the built site and holds its plot and its parts to what plays. |

## Adding a page

1. Write `src/pages/LEVEL/PAGE.ts` with `page({ … })` from `src/site/page.ts`, LEVEL one of `atoms`, `molecules`, `templates`, `shell`, `kobo` or `apps`; or with `foundation({ … })` under `foundations`. `src/pages/atoms/button.ts` and `src/pages/foundations/colour.ts` are the examples.
2. Add it to `PAGES` in `src/pages/index.ts`.
3. Add its line to `test/shots/surfaces`: `mockups/PAGE  design/mockups/src/pages/LEVEL/PAGE.ts`.

Every part is required, and the types refuse a page without one, or with an empty list where one is needed: `live` (the thing itself, beside the description), `description`, `variants`, `sizes`, `states`, `content` (text, a missing picture, none/one/many), `focus` (each line the part lights and its tone, and what lit makes of it by night and by day, `docs/DESIGN.md`, "States"), `motion` (arriving, leaving, between states, each move as data, below), `keyboard`, `measures` (the samples the overlay outlines, every measure by its token, and the reflow across window sizes). A part that doesn't apply is `doesntApply("why")`, and the page shows the reason in its place. The state matrix names the states it shows, draws them once by night and once by day, each grid painted in its mode, and wraps its columns onto bands when they are wider than the page; each of the others (rest, lit, pressed, disabled, selected, loading, empty, error) gets its reason in `absent`. Light and dark at every scale come from the toolbar on every page.

A foundation is one family of tokens, shown as it is used. It isn't a component, so it has no states, keyboard or focus line; its own parts are just as required: `live` and `description`, `tokens` (every token of the family, live, by its token), `rules` (each rule the values keep, with a worked example), `modes` (light beside dark), `themes` (every theme the toolbar paints in), `scales` (1, 1.25, 1.5 and 2, in device pixels), `content` (the longest word, a translation 40% longer), `motion` (moves as data, as a component's) and `checks` (what holds the family to its rules). Each shown part is a name, its words and a live sample. A foundation lists its tokens from the tokens' CSS (`names` in `src/lib/tokens.ts`), so a token added to `design/tokens.toml` shows on its page without the page changing; what the tokens say in words (a type style's use, where a colour role comes from, the contrast pairs) is a string there too, read with `words`.

A mock is built only from `src/lib`'s parts and the tokens: no number, colour or duration written out. A new mocked component is a module in `src/lib/` that wraps its root in `probe()` with its shared name, variant and fixed measures, as `kubaos-ui`'s `probe::component` does, so `just mockups-check` can hold the builds to it.

## Motion

A move is data, not prose: its `tracks`, one per part that moves (a control lighting up or pressed, the tube, the glow, the core, the colours, the lights), each naming the property it animates, its `from` and `to` (a number, or an opacity, colour or length token), its duration token, its cue after the move's start (a duration token, or the sum of several, one per phase before it), and its curve, an easing token, `neon.strike`'s steps or those steps played backwards. A track that moves every colour role (`--recolour`, 0 to 1) names its path and the two palettes; the path (`PATHS` in `src/site/recolour.ts`) says over which share of the fade each of lightness, chroma and hue moves. A move says what reduced motion plays (`reduced`: the same, or other tracks), what plays in light mode where it differs (`daylight`), what plays with the flicker off (`unflickered`, required of a move that flickers), a still closing beat that belongs to it (`tail`), and whether the loop plays the move back or cuts back to its first state. The speed setting divides every duration, cue and beat.

The player animates the sample with the Web Animations API from that same object (`src/site/recipe.ts`), so the recipe it shows is what plays. It loops: the first state held for `duration.scene`, the move, the last state held, then back. Under it are Pause, Restart, Slow ×10 and Copy as JSON, then the plot (`src/site/parts/plot.ts`), an SVG in the page's device pixels: the whole loop across in milliseconds, holds included, and each track's progress up, from its `from` (0) to its `to` (1), with room above 1 for a curve that overshoots (`pop`, `spring`). Each track is a curve from its cue for its duration: its easing token's cubic Bézier, or `neon.strike`'s steps, then back along the same curve, or cut back at the loop's end, as the move says. A track takes a colour and a dash, keyed in the legend and on its recipe row; a ball per track rides its curve at the vertical playhead, its height worked out from the curve as Chromium does. Dragging or clicking the plot holds that exact moment, computed from the keyframes rather than a CSS animation stopped midway; ← and → step one 60 Hz frame (16.67 ms), Shift with them ten, and the time and the frame show under it. The ticks under the x axis are each part's start and end and each keyframe, and clicking one goes to it. Reduced motion, light mode, the speed and the flicker draw the curves again for what plays then.

A move of more than one track renders its parts: under the legend, each part alone on a stage of its own, the sample drawn again with the other parts' layers hidden and only the controls the part is on, its layer animated by the same keyframes. Every stage, the plot and the recipe's rows run on the player's one clock, so pausing, seeking or stepping moves them all to the same frame. Pointing at a part's row, curve, legend key or stage picks it everywhere, and outlines its layers on the whole sample. In light mode a part its daylight tracks leave out keeps its row, its sample still, saying it doesn't play there, and the legend keeps the room of the other mode's, so a page is as tall in either mode.

The recipe follows as tables, the row of whatever is changing at the playhead lit, and the player's loop phase by phase. A shot (`?chrome=off`) holds every player on its first state. `node scripts/player-test.ts [PAGE…]`, run by `just mockups-check`, seeks every player on the built site with its own tracks, light mode's, reduced motion's, the flicker off's and at speed 2, and holds each ball to the progress the browser is playing (on a property two tracks share, the whole plays the later's), each part alone to the whole or to its own ball where a later track has the property, a tick to its moment and the arrow keys to a frame.

`just mockups-build` writes every page's recipes to `dist/motion/PAGE.json`, the moves as the libraries and a check read them.
